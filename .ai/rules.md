# Resume Scoring Rules & Red Flags

Use these guidelines to evaluate and improve the resume.

## The 20-Point Recruiter Skim Score
Score 1 point per item you can confidently say YES to. Four categories, five points each.

### 1. Format & Readability (5 points)
- [ ] 1 page (or max 2 if 10+ years / leadership)
- [ ] Clean layout — no tables, text boxes, or columns (ATS-safe)
- [ ] Bullets (not paragraphs) in Experience
- [ ] Consistent dates, titles, and spacing — no visual chaos
- [ ] Key info is left-aligned and obvious on first glance

### 2. Positioning & Target Role Clarity (5 points)
- [ ] Headline matches the role you're applying to (not "Professional Summary")
- [ ] Top ⅓ makes your level + lane obvious (function, niche, seniority)
- [ ] Recent experience supports the target role — not a miscellaneous dump
- [ ] Titles make sense to outsiders (or clarified with a parenthetical)
- [ ] No vague verbs — "helped / assisted / supported" used sparingly

### 3. Proof & Impact (5 points)
- [ ] Most bullets include outcomes — not just tasks
- [ ] Scope is clear (team size, budget, volume, pipeline, users)
- [ ] Numbers appear somewhere — even estimated ranges count
- [ ] Progression is visible (bigger scope, promotions, more responsibility)
- [ ] Tools, tech, and platforms relevant to the role are included

### 4. Keywords & Hiring Manager Fit (5 points)
- [ ] Mirrored job description language for top requirements
- [ ] Hard skills are easy to find — Skills section isn't fluff
- [ ] Experience uses hiring-manager language, not internal jargon
- [ ] No irrelevant clutter (old roles, outdated tools, random coursework)
- [ ] Most relevant wins appear in the first half of the page

## Evaluating Candidate Skills
When acting as an AI reading or modifying the resume:
1. Always calculate the "Recruiter Skim Score" (/20) before suggesting changes.
2. Be ruthless about cutting "filler" and "task-dumping" bullets.
3. If an engineering bullet lacks scale (users, latency drops, $ handled, team size), flag it immediately.

## 6 Red Flags That Get You Skipped
Avoid these patterns:
1. **Paragraph blocks in Experience**: Use 3 bullets max per block, starting with a strong verb + result.
2. **Task-dumping bullets (no outcomes)**: Focus on results (e.g., "Grew X to Y").
3. **Vague titles / unclear level**: Clarify titles (e.g., "Operations Associate (Project Coordination)").
4. **Missing scope**: Include team size, budget, or volume handled.
5. **Zero keywords from the JD**: Partner with stakeholders to deliver specific deliverables.
6. **"Skills" full of soft skills**: Focus on hard skills, tools, and platforms.
